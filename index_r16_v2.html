Signal Hierarchy

Primary — 2026 season form. Current team averages (scored, conceded) through Round 15 give a baseline expected combined. Calculated as (A avg scored + B avg conceded + B avg scored + A avg conceded) ÷ 2.

Secondary — H2H record vs actual bookmaker line (2025–26). How these teams have performed against the bookmaker's line at the time. Tracked as over/under rate and the average gap between actual combined and actual line. This round uses ausportsbetting.com historical data with real closing lines.

Tertiary — Line level vs H2H average combined. If the current line is significantly above (or below) the 3–5 year H2H average combined, that gap is a signal.

Confidence and Verdicts

VerdictConfidenceMeaning
STRONG BET75%+Multiple strong signals aligned. Highest conviction.
BET65–74%Clear directional signal with supporting evidence.
LEAN55–64%Moderate signal. One direction, not without risk.
WATCH45–54%No confident direction or signals conflict.

Multi rules

Multi legs are auto-selected at 80%+ confidence. The adjusted line is set at the point where 85–90%+ of historical H2H results land on the correct side. The 4-leg adjusted line multi is built from the four games with the clearest directional signal — each leg moves the line to a point where the historical hit rate is near certain. LEAN picks are never included in multis.

Check team lists Thursday/Friday before placing. Significant absences to first-choice halves, fullback, or starters can shift the model's expectation. Never put more on a multi than you're comfortable losing. Verify all adjusted lines and prices with your bookmaker — alternate totals market availability varies.
