Trend report

The season in three acts

The 2026 NRL season has split cleanly into three distinct scoring phases. Rounds 1–8 were the high-water mark: eight full rounds averaging 50.4 points per game, with R6 (58.0pts) and R8 (56.0pts) making OVER leans look straightforward. Teams were fit, summer conditions prevailed across most venues, and market lines hadn't fully caught up to the pace of scoring.

Rounds 12–16 — the Origin block — compressed scoring structurally. With 20–25 players unavailable each round, replacement-grade halves were directing attacks and team chemistry was fractured. The four Origin-disrupted rounds averaged 43.5 points per game across 24 games, a full 7-point drop from early-season pace. R15 coincided with the heaviest Origin withdrawal: just 5 games, averaging 44.2 points. The reduced fixtures in those rounds further skew any round-level analysis.

Rounds 17–18 have continued the decline despite Origin finishing. Winter conditions across southern venues have taken over as the primary suppressor. R17 was the season's lowest-scoring round at 38.5 points per game. Two of R17's eight games produced totals under 35, driven by wet conditions and error rates running well above the seasonal norm. The model's WATCH OVER lean in the Knights v Tigers game (18 total, OVER 51.5 line) was the most instructive miss of the season.

Structural takeaway: The market set early-season lines around 48–52 points. Post-Origin and into winter, the equilibrium has shifted to 40–46. Any game with a line above 48 in Rounds 19–25 should be scrutinised hard before an OVER lean is published.

What the ruck infringement data tells us

Across all 131 games this season, NRL.com recorded 844 ruck infringements — the stat that maps directly to 6-again restart calls. The season average is 6.4 per game, but the distribution is the more interesting story.

The within-game signal is confirmed. Games with 10 or more combined ruck infringements consistently produce lower totals. The R17 sample validated this cleanly — both sub-35pt games had exactly 10 RI each, 70% above the round average. At the individual game level, high RI volume correlates with defensive dominance and slowed play-the-ball tempo, both of which suppress scoring.¥Ïp>

The across-round signal is much weaker. R2 had the season's highest RI average (9.1 per game) but was not a low-scoring round — teams were converting their extra sets. Origin-disrupted rounds averaged 5.5 RI per game versus 6.7 for full-strength rounds, consistent with reduced intensity rather than any change in refereeing.

Practical implication: Do not use round-level RI totals from prior weeks as a forward-looking input. Track per-game RI weekly as a calibration data point. When pre-game conditions suggest high RI volume is likely (wet conditions, two defensive teams, high error-rate form), treat it as a supporting signal for an UNDER lean — not a primary driver.

Weather and venue: the unquantified edge

The season's sharpest lessons have come from weather events the model didn't price correctly. R17 across multiple venues produced conditions (wet, 12–15°C) that amplified error rates and compressed scoring in ways that H2H history alone can't predict. Outdoor southern venues — McDonald Jones Stadium, GIO Stadium, Accor Stadium, Netstrata Jubilee — are materially more weather-sensitive than Suncorp, Queensland Country Bank, or Allegiant Stadium.

The data to build a proper venue × month adjustment table is accumulating from R15 onward. The target is a simple lookup: if venue is [southern outdoor] and month is [June–August] and forecast includes rain, apply a -4 to -6pt adjustment to the implied total before comparing to the market line. This alone would have prevented the R17 WATCH OVER publication.

Key model findings

R17 WATCH OVER miss · Knights v Tigers (18 total vs OVER 51.5)
28 combined errors in wet conditions at McDonald Jones Stadium. Both lowest-scoring R17 games had 10 ruck infringements each — 70% above the round average. Error rate was the primary driver, not team quality or form. Confirms: WATCH OVER leans in wet/cold conditions should not be published.
WATCH OTER bKans carry disproportionate risk
Season hit rate excl. WATCH: 75% (6/8). Season hit rate incl. WATCH: 69% (9/13). The model's structural edge sits on the UNDER side — error rate, weather, and defensive sets all act as natural suppressors. WATCH OVER leans fight against this edge and should be reviewed before publishing.
Season scoring has declined materially since R8
R1–R8 average: 50.4pts. R17–R18 average: 39.7pts. A 10.7pt drop across the season. The model's historical 40–44pt baseline is more applicable now than early-season. Market lines set during early rounds may lag this adjustment — lean UNDER in winter rounds where lines remain elevated.
Origin rounds are structurally lower-scoring
Origin-disrupted rounds (R12, R13, R15, R16) averaged 43.5pts across 24 games vs 48.7pts for non-Origin rounds. Top-line playmakers absent, replacement-grade halves, disrupted team chemistry. Apply a downward adjustment to any game involving Origin-selected teams in Origin weeks. Reduced fixture in those rounds also skews round-level analysis.

Model improvement opportunities

Priority Suppress WATCH OVER leans in winter conditions
Rule: if weather forecast includes rain/wet or temperature below 12°C, do not publish WATCH OVER leans. HIGH confidence OVER leans can still publish — the threshold should be raised for WATCH. The variance suppression from wet conditions consistently outweighs the upward scoring signal.
Priority Add season-phase adjustment to baseline total
The model currently uses a fixed historical baseline (40–44pts). A rolling 4-week average of actual game totals would make the baseline dynamic. Implement: track last 4 rounds' average as "current baseline" and weight it alongside H2H history. This would have caught the R17 downward trend earlier.
Consider Origin round flag
Add detection logic: if a game involves teams with multiple Origin representatives and the round falls on an Origin match week, apply a -3 to -5pt downward adjustment to the implied total. Track which teams are most affected (QLD-heavy: Broncos, Cowboys, Storm; NSW-heavy: Panthers, Roosters). Do not apply to Origin-adjacent rounds where players have returned.
Confirmed Ruck infringement as within-game signal
Full-season data (131 games, 844 total RI, 6.4/gm) confirms: games with ≥10 ruck infringements consistently correlate with lower scoring. Across-round RI volume is noise — do not use prior-round RI totals as a pre-game input. Track per-game RI as a weekly post-game calibration point. Origin rounds show lower RI (5.5/gm vs 6.7/gm), consistent with reduced intensity.
Consider Venue × winter factor
Outdoor southern venues (McDonald Jones, GIO, Accor, Netstrata) in wet/cold conditions consistently produce lower totals. Heated or Queensland venues (Suncorp, QCB) are less affected. Build a venue × month adjustment table from R15 onward. Target: a simple -4 to -6pt lookup for southern outdoor venues in June–August with rain forecast.
Future Error rate as pre-game signal
Post-game errors correlate with lower totals, but can we predict high-error games pre-game? Candidate inputs: recent 4-week error rate per team (NRL.com Team Stats), fatigue indicators (back-to-back away games, post-Origin returns). Medium-term enhancement requiring consistent data collection starting now.
