About the model

Confidence tiers (HIGH / SOLID / WATCH / PASS) are based on what percentage of recent head-to-head results finished under the actual Sportsbet market line — not a fixed threshold like 60.

HIGH = 85%+ of recent results under the market line. SOLID = 70–84%. WATCH = 50–69% or volatility risk. PASS = no clear edge.

Multi recommendations include only HIGH and SOLID tier games. WATCH games are never included regardless of how close they are to the threshold.
