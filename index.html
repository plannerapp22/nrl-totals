Season phase

Post-Origin rebound continues — plus a methodology fix
The +2pt rebound uplift and tightened tier thresholds (HIGH needs +6.5, SOLID needs +4) stay in force through R24. Separately: we found and fixed a real bias in the tier system. HIGH and SOLID had only ever been awarded to unders since Round 15 — a genuinely strong OVER signal had nowhere to go and got filed as a generic WATCH note regardless of how clear the data was. Re-checking last round's WATCH-leans-OVER calls against the plain H2H hit rate (not just the recency-weighted average) showed most of them were actually closer to a coin flip than a real lean — the weighted average was getting pulled around by one or two recent blowouts. From this round on, genuine over-signals get a dedicated Shootout Risk flag: a warning that the standard under is unsafe, paired with a suggested alternate-totals Over range well below the line, rather than a straight bet-the-over call. The core model's edge stays in finding safe unders — this doesn't change that, it just stops burying the real risk cases in noise.
