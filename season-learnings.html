Key Model Findings

R17 WATCH OVER miss — Knights v Tigers (18 total vs OVER 51.5)
Investigated game: 28 combined errors (14 each vs season avg ~11.7/team) in wet conditions at McDonald Jones Stadium. Ground Conditions: Wet. The error rate was the primary driver — hot team quality or form. Both teams also produced 10 combined ruck infringements. This was a variance event compounded by weather, not a systematic model failure. However it confirms the risk of WATCH OVER leans in winter conditions.
WATCH OVER leans carry disproportionate risk
Season hit rate excl. WATCH: 75% (6/8). Season hit rate incl. WATCH: 69% (9/13). WATCH OVER leans have underperformed. The model's structural edge sits on the UNDER side — error rate, weather, and defensive sets all act as natural suppressors. WATCH OVER leans fight against this edge and should be reviewed before publishing.
Season scoring has declined materially from R1–R8 peak
R1–R8 season average: 50.4pts. R17–R18 average: 39.7pts. A 10.7pt drop across the season. The model's historical baseline of 40–44pts is more applicable to the current period than early-season. Market lines set during early rounds may have lagged this adjustment — an opportunity the model can exploit by leaning toward UNDER in winter rounds where lines remain elevated.
Origin rounds are structurally lower-scoring
Rounds affected by State of Origin (R12, R13, R15, R16) averaged 43.5pts across 24 games — vs 48.7pts for non-Origin rounds. Cause: top-line playmakers absent, replacement-grade halves, disrupted team structure and chemistry. Model should apply a downward adjustment to any game involving Origin-selected teams in Origin weeks. Fixture reduction in those rounds (5–7 games vs 8) also skews round-level analysis.

Model Improvement Opportunities

Priority actions Priority
Priority Suppress WATCH OVER leans in winter conditions
Implement a rule: if weather forecast includes rain/wet or temperature below 12°C, do not publish WATCH OVER leans. The variance suppression from wet conditions consistently outweighs the upward scoring signal. HIGH confidence OVER leans can still publish — the threshold should be raised for WATCH.
Priority Add season-phase adjustment to baseline total
The model currently uses a fixed historical baseline (40–44pts). A rolling 4-week average of actual game totals would make the baseline dynamic. Implement: track last 4 rounds' average as "current baseline" and weight it alongside H2H history. This would have caught the R17 downward trend earlier.
Consider Origin round flag
Add detection logic: if a game involves teams with multiple Origin representatives and the round falls on an Origin match week, apply a -3 to -5pt downward adjustment to the implied total. Track which teams are most affected (QLD-heavy teams like Broncos, Cowboys, Storm; NSW-heavy teams like Panthers, Roosters). Do not apply to Origin-adjacent rounds where players have returned.
Consider Ruck infringement rate as a within-game signal — confirmed
Full-season data (131 games, 844 total RI, 6.4/gm average) confirms: the within-game signal holds. Games with ≥10 ruck infringements consistently correlate with lower scoring — validated across the season. Across-round correlation is weaker (R2 had the most RI at 9.1/gm but was not a low-scoring round). Recommend: track per-game RI as a weekly post-game data point. Do not use round-level RI totals as a pre-game input — the between-round signal is noise. Origin rounds show lower RI (5.5/gm vs 6.7/gm for full rounds), consistent with reduced intensity.
Consider Venue winter factor
Outdoor southern venues (McDonald Jones, GIO, Accor, Netstrata) in wet/cold conditions consistently produce lower totals. Heated/indoor or Queensland venues (Suncorp, QCB) are less affected. Build a venue × month adjustment table once sufficient seasonal data is available. Start tracking from R15 onward.
Future Error rate as pre-game signal
Post-game errors correlate with lower totals, but can we predict high-error games pre-game? Candidate inputs: recent 4-week error rate per team (from NRL.com Team Stats), plus fatigue indicators (back-to-back away games, post-Origin returns). This is a medium-term model enhancement requiring consistent data collection starting now.
