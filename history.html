About the model

Confidence tiers (HIGH / SOLID / WATCH / PASS) are based on H2H history, 2026 season form, and market line analysis — not a fixed scoring threshold.

HIGH = strongest signal (H2H and form aligned, gap vs line 6pts+; +6.5 in the post-Origin rebound phase). SOLID = clear edge (gap 3–5.9pts, majority of signals agree; +4 in rebound phase). WATCH = marginal or volatile — shown as advisory only, never counted in hit rate. PASS = no clear edge.

Multi recommendations are built from HIGH-tier legs only from R20 onwards (HIGH and SOLID prior to R20). WATCH games are never included regardless of proximity to threshold. In the post-Origin rebound phase, no multi is forced if fewer than two HIGH legs exist.

Every round is tagged with its season phase (Origin window / post-Origin rebound / run home) and the model tracks expectation error per phase — the line-lag detector feeding the over-module decision, reviewed late July 2026.
