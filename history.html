📈 Key season finding

The 2026 NRL is running historically low-scoring. Of 121 completed games (R1–R16 + Eels/Rabbitohs R17), 95 (78.5%) finished under 60 total points. Only 21 games exceeded 60 — and most of those were blowouts by attacking-heavy teams (Cowboys, Roosters, Dolphins at home). A blanket UNDER strategy vs 60 would be profitable nearly every round. The model's job is to filter for the highest-confidence spots and avoid the predictable blowout matchups.

Rounds to note: R3, R12, R13 were 100% under 60. R6 was the worst round for unders — 4 of 8 games went over.
