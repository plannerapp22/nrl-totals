Methodology change · 23 Jul 2026

The tier system had a bias, and we've fixed it. Since Round 15, HIGH and SOLID had only ever been awarded to UNDER leans — the tier definitions literally scored games on "% under," so a genuinely strong OVER signal had no tier to go to and got filed as a generic WATCH note, however clear the data actually was (Round 21's Cowboys v Broncos — H2H cleared the line in 67% of meetings since 2022, sd 10.6 — was about to be published as an unremarkable WATCH before this was caught).

We considered making HIGH/SOLID fully symmetric for overs too, and decided against it. The product's real edge is finding safe unders — a mirrored overs tier would dilute that and turn this into something it isn't. Instead, genuine over-signals now get a dedicated Shootout Risk flag: a warning that the standard under is unsafe in that game, paired with a suggested alternate-totals Over range well below the market line (roughly line minus 12 to minus 18) rather than a straight bet-the-over call at even-money risk. It's gated to real conviction only (plain H2H hit-rate ≤35% under, n≥6, sd≤16, not contradicted by season market tendency) — expect zero to two flags most rounds, not a parallel tier system.

One knock-on fix: game cards now show the plain H2H hit-rate (count under the line ÷ games) alongside the recency-weighted average, not just the weighted figure. Several Round 20/21 "leans OVER" WATCH calls turned out to be near-even or actually under-favoured on the plain count once checked — the weighted average alone was getting pulled around by one or two recent blowouts. This doesn't change any graded HIGH/SOLID record (those were always unders and stay unders), but it does mean some of the WATCH commentary language in earlier rounds was overstating over-signals that weren't really there.

About the model

Confidence tiers (HIGH / SOLID / WATCH / PASS) are based on H2H history, 2026 season form, and market line analysis — not a fixed scoring threshold. HIGH and SOLID are reserved for UNDER leans only — that's the model's actual edge and the only bets it grades a track record on.

HIGH = strongest signal (H2H and form aligned, gap vs line 6pts+; +6.5 in the post-Origin rebound phase). SOLID = clear edge (gap 3–5.9pts, majority of signals agree; +4 in rebound phase). WATCH = marginal, volatile, or a genuine over-lean — shown as advisory only, never counted in hit rate. PASS = no clear edge.

Shootout Risk (added Round 21) is a separate, non-tier warning for games where backing the standard under would be a bad bet — it comes with a suggested alternate-totals Over range instead of a graded pick, and is never included in the multi. None triggered in Round 22 or Round 23.

Multi recommendations are built from HIGH-tier UNDER legs only from R20 onwards (HIGH and SOLID prior to R20). WATCH games and Shootout Risk flags are never included regardless of proximity to threshold. In the post-Origin rebound phase, no multi is forced if fewer than two HIGH legs exist — that's been the case in R20, R21 and R22, and again in R23 (one HIGH leg only).

Every round is tagged with its season phase (Origin window / post-Origin rebound / run home) and the model tracks expectation error per phase.
