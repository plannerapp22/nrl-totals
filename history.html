About The Bias

Why everything leans under

R17 shows all 8 games leaning under. Some of that is real — this is a genuinely low-scoring round on the numbers. But the model also has structural tendencies worth knowing:

  • The threshold is fixed at 60. H2H percentages measure games that finished under 60 points — not under the actual betting line. A game the model expects to finish 52 looks like a strong "under 60" signal even if the market line is 48.5 and the game finishes 50 (an over).
  • Historical averages skew low. The 2022–2024 era had lower game totals than 2025–2026. H2H averages in the 38–45 range are being compared to a 2026 game that markets expect to finish around 48–52.
  • No lines this run. Without actual Sportsbet lines, every lean is vs the model’s own expectation — not the market price. You can’t bet against a line that isn’t there.
  • Stale form data. R13 fallback misses 3–4 rounds of games that could shift the picture.

Best practice: treat HIGH vs SOLID as relative tiers within the week. Always check the actual line. If the line is already 46, “model expects 43” might be priced in.
